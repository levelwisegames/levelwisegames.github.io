How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to master. Every puzzle can be solved with pure logic.

Hang every weight

Drag weights from your tray onto the hooks until every scale hangs level.

Distance counts

A scale balances when weight × arm length is equal on both sides.

Scales on scales

A sub-scale counts as everything hanging on it. Think it through.

Five worlds to travel

A new world every 24 levels.
