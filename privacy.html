Privacy Policy – Equilibria

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how Equilibria (the "game"), a mobile puzzle game published by Levelwise Games ("we", "us"), handles information. We keep it simple: the game itself does not ask for, collect or send any personal information to us. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, hint count and settings (such as sound on/off) only on your device. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. This includes optional rewarded videos (watched in exchange for a hint) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and used by Google according to its own policies. You can learn more here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Your choices and consent

  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: when required by law, the game shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, where you can accept or choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's home screen.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose whether to watch one for an extra hint.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information. The game does not use in-app purchases or our own analytics.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

We do not store any of your personal information on our servers. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies linked above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at levelwisegames@gmail.com.
